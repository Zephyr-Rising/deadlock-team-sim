# Deadlock Team Synergy

**Live page:** https://zephyr-rising.github.io/deadlock-team-sim/

Put six Deadlock heroes into three lanes and see how the team's estimated win chance moves:

1. **Heroes on their own**: each hero's strength, from a logistic model of every match (both line-ups).
2. **Pair synergy**: for each of the 15 pairs, how often their team won compared with what all twelve heroes' strengths predicted. Pairs with fewer games are pulled toward zero, and the sum over 15 pairs is rescaled so it matches held-out matches.
3. **Lane partners**: the extra effect of the two heroes in a lane laning together. It turns out to be tiny on win chance, so the laning phase is shown in **souls at 15:00** instead. That's each duo's expected soul gap against the duo it faced, split into the two heroes' lane strength and the duo's own synergy.

The page also suggests the best next pick for any slot and lists one hero's best and worst partners.

## Data

- ~1.5M Ranked and Unranked Normal 6v6 matches from the public [deadlock-api.com](https://deadlock-api.com) match lake.
- Excluded: matches with a leaver and matches shorter than 10 minutes.
- The date window is shown on the page.
- All settings were chosen on half of the matches and checked on the other half; the checks are at the bottom of the page.

`data.json` is generated by `team_synergy.py` (kept outside this repo). Rerun it after a patch to refresh the numbers.

These numbers show which picks have gone together with winning. They don't prove cause: players' skill, duos who queue together and counter-picks against the enemy aren't separated out.
